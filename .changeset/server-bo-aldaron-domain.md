The Aldaron team can have its own back-office address. An employee invited with Aldaron-only roles now receives a link to that address, and the invitation e-mail carries the Aldaron name and website instead of the portfolio's. Opened on that address, the back-office starts in the Aldaron workspace; an account that only holds the portfolio workspace still stays in the portfolio. Nothing changes until the optional `AGENCY_SERVER_URL`, `AGENCY_WEBSITE_URL`, `AGENCY_APP_NAME` (server) and `VITE_AGENCY_HOSTNAME` (back-office) are set. The e-mail logo is still the portfolio's.
