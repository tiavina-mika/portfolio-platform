The portfolio and Aldaron back-offices each have their own two-factor authentication, and a sign-in opens
only the workspace of the address it was made on. To work in the other workspace, sign in on its address.
Enabling or disabling it signs out the other sessions of that workspace only.
Once the setup window is closed, the page behind it loads its data again.
