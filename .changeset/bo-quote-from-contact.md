A quote request sent from the Aldaron site is now filed in the Aldaron workspace: the contacts page lists the messages of the active workspace only, and a message of a workspace the account cannot open reads as not found. Under an Aldaron request, "Create the client and the quote" opens the quote form prefilled (the client holding the same e-mail, or a new one made from the request; the product as the title; one line at 0 per line of the estimate). Once saved, the request names its quote and cannot become a second one. No migration: contacts saved before carry no target and stay in the portfolio workspace. The site's form does not change for the visitor.
