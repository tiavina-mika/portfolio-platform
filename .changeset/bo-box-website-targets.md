Articles, pages and categories can now be assigned to the websites they are written for: Portfolio, Box, or both. A Box-only content is meant for the Aldaron website. When Box is picked, the form asks what the box includes (static website, mobile, back-office, web application), and optionally its price in euros and its duration in days.

The public article read accepts an optional website: the portfolio keeps the articles saved before the field existed, the box keeps only the ones that name it.
