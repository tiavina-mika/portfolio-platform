What an Aldaron employee and the owner can do now, once the Aldaron domain is configured:

- An employee invited with Aldaron-only roles receives a link to the Aldaron domain, not the portfolio's.
- The invitation e-mail carries the Aldaron name and links to the Aldaron website.
- The back-office opened on the Aldaron domain starts in the Aldaron workspace.
- Invitations to portfolio roles, and to the highest roles, are unchanged.

Nothing changes until the Aldaron variables are set.
