# portfolio-server

## 1.1.0

### Minor Changes

- 70dfd5d: The Aldaron workspace gets its first business list: the clients. In the Aldaron workspace a new "Clients" sidebar entry lists, creates, edits and archives them (an archived client can be put back); one e-mail address, whatever its case, is one client. The entry is hidden in the portfolio workspace, and the server refuses every client function to an account whose roles do not open Aldaron, whatever its rights on clients. The `Client` class is reached through its Cloud Functions only: its class-level permissions never mirror the role rights. A role can now be saved with rights on a domain class (`Project`, `Client`), which the server used to refuse. No migration: the class and its indexes are created at startup.
- e72c9c4: The Aldaron workspace gets its missions. A new "Missions" sidebar entry, shown only in the Aldaron workspace, lists the work done for the clients by status, with a search on the title and "My missions" for the ones given to the signed-in user. A mission is written for an active client, optionally for one of its accepted quotes, given to a member of a role that opens the Aldaron workspace, with an optional phase of its status, dates and a description; "Create the mission" on an accepted quote opens it prefilled with the client, the title and the quote. Its status only moves forward (planned, in progress, done, or cancelled), starting or finishing it fills an empty start or end date, the phase is cleared on a status change, and the client and the quote no longer change once it started. A client that has missions can no longer be deleted. No migration: the `Mission` class and its indexes are created at startup.
- 33bb576: An accepted Aldaron quote gets its payments, on one schedule for every quote: a 30 % deposit due on the day of the acceptance, a 40 % milestone and a 30 % balance, the balance absorbing the rounding so the three always add up to the total. They are written once, by the acceptance; a quote that is not accepted has none. "Payments" on an accepted quote shows the total, what is paid and what is left, and each payment with its status (planned, due, paid, cancelled). A payment is marked as paid by hand with its method, an optional transfer reference and its day (today by default, never ahead); the milestone and the balance are dated once known; a mistake is corrected by cancelling the payment, which writes a new unpaid one of the same part in its place. A paid payment is never edited, no payment is ever deleted, and the quote's paid amount follows the paid payments. A quote accepted before this change gets its schedule from "Create the payment schedule". No migration: the `Payment` class and its indexes are created at startup.
- ca19963: The Aldaron workspace gets its quotes. A new "Quotes" sidebar entry, shown only in the Aldaron workspace, lists the quotes by status and writes a draft for an active client (a client can be created from the quote form), with its lines, its currency (euro or ariary), an optional VAT rate and its total computed while typing. Each quote gets a readable number `AL-<year>-<NNN>`, gap-free and restarting each year. A draft is edited; it is then marked sent, and accepted (with how the client accepted), refused or expired: no other move is allowed, and a quote that left draft can no longer be edited. The number, the total and the paid amount are computed by the server and refused as input. A default currency, in the languages section of the settings, only prefills a new quote. A client that has quotes can no longer be deleted. No migration: the `Quote` and `Counter` classes and their indexes are created at startup.
- d593101: Articles, pages and categories can now be assigned to the websites they are written for: Portfolio, Box, or both. A Box-only content is meant for the Aldaron website. When Box is picked, the form asks what the box includes (static website, mobile, back-office, web application), and optionally its price in euros and its duration in days.

  The public article read accepts an optional website: the portfolio keeps the articles saved before the field existed, the box keeps only the ones that name it.

  The portfolio website only shows the articles, pages and categories written for the portfolio (the ones saved before the field existed included), in the blog, the pages, the categories, the sitemap and the search.

- 53d19a5: Accepting a quote can now join one proof file (the signed quote or the client's e-mail, as a PDF or an image, 5 MB at most), stored with the other back-office files; the quote list then offers an "Acceptance proof" link under the status of that quote. The server takes a proof only with the acceptance itself, and only a file uploaded to the platform's own proofs folder. No migration: older accepted quotes simply have no proof.
- 37e5b7e: A quote request sent from the Aldaron site is now filed in the Aldaron workspace: the contacts page lists the messages of the active workspace only, and a message of a workspace the account cannot open reads as not found. Under an Aldaron request, "Create the client and the quote" opens the quote form prefilled (the client holding the same e-mail, or a new one made from the request; the product as the title; one line at 0 per line of the estimate). Once saved, the request names its quote and cannot become a second one. No migration: contacts saved before carry no target and stay in the portfolio workspace. The site's form does not change for the visitor.
- 0675097: A sent quote can now be revised: "Revise" creates a draft copy under the same number with the next version (v2, v3…), and the sent version becomes expired. An accepted quote can be completed by an amendment: "Create an amendment" opens the quote form prefilled with its client, currency, VAT rate and terms, and the amendment gets its own number. The quote list shows the version and the quote each revision or amendment comes from. The server decides from the status of that quote (sent: revision, accepted: amendment), keeps its client, and never revises an accepted quote. No migration: existing quotes are first versions with no parent.
- 2b52ea5: Deleting an account from the users list now asks for a reason (details required for "other"), shown in the recycle bin, the audit logs and the user page, and the deletion is recorded as made by the administrator. The recycle bin can erase accounts for good, from a row or a selection, and an erased account leaves the bin while its deletion log stays.
- ce0d381: The field saying where an article, a page or a category is published is now `targets` (was `websites`), with the values `portfolio` and `agency` (was `box`). The offer details asked for Aldaron content are `offerIncludes`, `offerPrice` and `offerDurationDays` (were `boxIncludes`, `boxPrice`, `boxDurationDays`), and the public article read takes a `target` instead of a `website`.

  Run `renameContentTargetFieldsJob` once right after deploying: it moves the existing rows to the new names, and the value `aldaron` stored on articles, pages, categories, projects and roles to `agency`. Until it has run, content kept for Aldaron alone is read as the portfolio's.

- 6af217e: The back-office works in one of two workspaces, the portfolio or Aldaron, picked in Settings by anyone whose roles open both. The Aldaron logo replaces the portfolio one in the sidebar, the website forms of Settings (details, main images, CV) are hidden, and the lists of projects, articles, pages and categories follow the workspace. A role has a new `workspaces` field (the portfolio when left out; the highest roles open both), and the server refuses an Aldaron list or detail to an account whose roles do not open it. No migration is needed: a role saved before the field opens the portfolio only.
- ae7316f: A project now belongs to one website: the portfolio (the default) or Aldaron, never both. The project form has a "Published on" select in its settings, saved in the new `target` field.

  The public project read accepts an optional `target`. The portfolio website only shows the projects published on the portfolio (the ones saved before the field existed included), in the list, the project page, the sitemap and the search.

- 5c56d23: The Aldaron team can have its own back-office address. An employee invited with Aldaron-only roles now receives a link to that address, and the invitation e-mail carries the Aldaron name and website instead of the portfolio's. Opened on that address, the back-office starts in the Aldaron workspace; an account that only holds the portfolio workspace still stays in the portfolio. Nothing changes until the optional `AGENCY_SERVER_URL`, `AGENCY_WEBSITE_URL`, `AGENCY_APP_NAME` (server) and `VITE_AGENCY_HOSTNAME` (back-office) are set. The Aldaron workspace can also send its e-mails through its own Brevo account (`AGENCY_BREVO_API_KEY` and `AGENCY_ADMIN_EMAIL_SENDER`, set together). It can also store its files in its own Cloudinary account (`AGENCY_CLOUDINARY_NAME`, `AGENCY_CLOUDINARY_API_KEY`, `AGENCY_CLOUDINARY_API_SECRET`, all three together): the back-office sends the workspace it is in with each upload, and a file is later deleted from the account named in its address. Once `AGENCY_APP_NAME` is set, the e-mails of the Aldaron workspace use the `agency-logo` Cloudinary asset, read from the Aldaron account when it has one, which must contain it.
- 205f7ea: The contact form of the website has an optional phone number field, saved with the message and shown in the back-office, and the home projects title now reads "Mes dernières réalisations".
- f5ad5a3: A page or an article can show a clickable table of contents of its section titles on the website, turned on with a new "Sommaire" switch in the settings of its back-office form, and the content editor of the back-office now lists the titles of the text next to it.

### Patch Changes

- 9b29256: Three default roles open the Aldaron workspace only (team, team lead and administrator), the addresses of AGENCY_OWNERS_EMAILS now receive the Aldaron administrator role instead of the owner one, and a second back-office dev server (`dev:agency`) can stand for the Aldaron workspace on its own port.
- 00f3d84: The Aldaron owners can be listed apart from the portfolio ones and are invited from the Aldaron domain with its e-mail frame, and an administrator with no role who opens the back-office on the Aldaron domain is given the Aldaron address to write to.
- 2b835c4: An administrator can no longer delete their own account from the users list, nor the account of an owner or a super administrator unless they hold one of those roles themselves, nor the last active owner: the trash icon is hidden on one's own row and a selection containing such an account is refused as a whole, with a message.
- ed526a2: An editor whose roles open only one workspace can no longer change, create or move an article, a page, a category or a project that belongs to the other one.
- 75bd4fc: The user list, the user detail and the recent accounts of the dashboard no longer show the staff of the portfolio to an account whose roles open the Aldaron workspace only.
- 301f657: The global search, the badges and the bulk actions of the projects, and the project counts of the dashboard, now stay inside the workspaces the account's roles open.
- 5517805: Sign-in and sign-up routes are now limited per account identifier on top of a wider cap per address, a client can no longer claim to be a back-office account or move the sign-up step of an account that confirmed no contact, a new password may not be the email or phone number nor contain a word of the email (with translated messages for each rule), the server refuses to boot when the static encryption key is not exactly 32 bytes, and an account deleted from the users list leaves the list right away.
- 0517fe0: Slack is no longer used: the morning summary and the failed-job message are gone, and staff alerts are sent by e-mail only.
